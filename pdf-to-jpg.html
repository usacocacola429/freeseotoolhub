Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF to JPG converter really free?

Yes, 100% free forever. No signup, no watermarks, no file limits, no hidden charges. Even batch conversion and password-protected PDFs are free.

Are my PDFs safe and private?

Absolutely. All conversion happens locally in your browser using PDF.js. Your files never leave your device and are never uploaded to any server.

Can I convert password-protected PDFs?

Yes. Enter the password in the settings field and click Convert. The PDF will be unlocked locally in your browser — we never see your password.

What DPI should I choose?

72 DPI for web/email (smaller files), 150 DPI for standard use (recommended), 300 DPI for print-quality images, 450 DPI for maximum detail. Higher DPI = larger files.

Can I convert specific pages only?

Yes. Use the page range field to convert specific pages. Examples: "1-5" (pages 1 to 5), "1,3,5" (pages 1, 3, 5), "1-5,8,12-15" (mixed). Leave empty to convert all pages.

What image format should I choose?

JPG is best for photos and smaller files. PNG preserves quality but larger size (and supports transparency). WEBP offers the best compression at good quality.
