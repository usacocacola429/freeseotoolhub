Frequently Asked Questions

How does this tool work?
It scans your content for keywords from an editable keyword map (default: common SEO terms). It then shows frequency, density, context sentences, and suggests internal link URLs. Everything runs in your browser.
Can I use my own keywords and URLs?
Yes! Click "Edit Keywords" to customize the keyword → URL map. Add your own pages, keywords, and URLs. Changes are saved in your browser.
What is keyword density?
Keyword density = (number of times keyword appears / total words) × 1000. It shows how often a keyword appears per 1000 words. Optimal internal link density is 2-5 links per 1000 words.
Is my content stored?
No. Everything runs 100% in your browser. Your content and keyword map stay on your device.
