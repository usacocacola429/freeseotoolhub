Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between PNG and SVG?
PNG is a raster format — great for screen. SVG is vector — scales infinitely without quality loss, perfect for print (business cards, posters, banners).
Can I use these QR codes commercially?
Yes, 100%. No watermark, no licensing fees. Use them for business, marketing, print materials — anything.
Is my data private?
Absolutely. Everything runs 100% in your browser. Your content, logo, and QR codes never leave your device.
What's the best QR size for printing?
Minimum 2×2 cm for close scanning, 3×3 cm for a business card, 10×10 cm for a poster. Always download SVG for print — it scales without blur.
