Frequently Asked Questions

What are meta tags?

HTML elements that tell search engines and social platforms about your page content. Essential for SEO and social sharing.

What is hreflang?

A tag that tells Google which language/region version of a page to show. Essential for multi-language sites — prevents duplicate content penalties and improves rankings in local Google indexes.

What is og:locale?

Open Graph locale (e.g. en_US, de_DE) that tells Facebook, LinkedIn, and other platforms which language your page uses — so previews show the right language.

Pixel vs Character Length?

Google truncates based on pixel width, not characters. Titles: ~580px. Descriptions: ~990px. CJK languages (Japanese, Chinese) fit fewer characters in the same width.

What is JSON-LD?

Structured data in JSON format that helps Google understand your content for rich snippets (ratings, prices, FAQs).
