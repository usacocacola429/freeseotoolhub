Frequently Asked Questions

What are meta tags?

HTML elements that tell search engines and social platforms about your page content. Essential for SEO and social sharing.

Pixel vs Character Length?

Google truncates based on pixel width, not characters. Titles: ~580px. Descriptions: ~990px. Our tool counts both.

What is JSON-LD?

Structured data in JSON format that helps Google understand your content for rich snippets (ratings, prices, FAQs).

Why Open Graph + Twitter separately?

Facebook uses OG tags; Twitter can use its own. Best practice: include both for maximum coverage.

Is this tool free?

Yes, 100% free with no signup, no limits, no API key. Everything runs in your browser.

How to implement?

Copy the HTML code and paste it into the <head> section of your page, before </head>.
