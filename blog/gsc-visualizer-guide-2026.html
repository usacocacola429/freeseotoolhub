GSC Metrics You Should Track

Our GSC Visualizer supports all 6 core Search Console metrics. Here's what each one tells you:

Clicks
Total number of times users clicked your site in search results.
Impressions
How often your site appeared in search results (whether clicked or not).
CTR
Click-Through Rate — clicks ÷ impressions. Higher = more compelling titles.
Avg. Position
Your average ranking position. Lower is better (1 = top).
Top Queries
Search terms driving traffic. Your keyword goldmine.
Top Pages
Which of your pages perform best in search.

Tips for Getting the Most from GSC Visualizer

  • Export monthly: Download your GSC CSVs at the same time each month for clean comparison.
  • Track trends, not snapshots: Look at quarter-over-quarter, not just this week.
  • Watch CTR + position together: A page ranking #3 with low CTR means your title/description needs work. Test with our SERP Simulator.
  • Find rising queries: Queries with high impressions but low clicks are golden opportunities — improve your title.
  • Audit worst pages: Pages with high impressions but low position need content improvement. Use our Keyword Density Checker to optimize them.
  • Check device split: If mobile CTR is much lower than desktop, your mobile UX needs work. Test with our Mobile-Friendly Test.
