Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions here.

How can I suggest a new tool? +

You can suggest a new tool by using the form above (select "New Tool Suggestion") or emailing us at suggestions@freeseotoolhub.com.

Please include the tool's purpose, key features, and target audience. We carefully review all suggestions.

What should I do if I find a bug? +

Please include a screenshot (if possible), your browser name and version, and a description of when the issue occurred. Email us at bugs@freeseotoolhub.com.

Will your tools always be free? +

Yes, all our core tools will remain free forever. We fund the site through advertising, sponsorships, and optionally premium features — but the basic toolkit will never be locked behind a paywall.

How can I partner with FreeSEOToolHub? +

We're open to collaborations in content, development, marketing, and translation. Send your portfolio and proposal to contact@freeseotoolhub.com.

How can I support FreeSEOToolHub? +

Use our tools, share them with friends, follow us on social media, and provide feedback. Every bit of support helps us keep improving!
