Frequently Asked Questions

How do I export from Google Search Console?
Open Search Console → Performance → click "Export" → choose "CSV". You can export Dates, Queries, Pages, Countries, and Devices separately.
Which files should I upload?
Upload any combination. The tool auto-detects each file's type by its column headers. Dates file gives the timeline chart, others give ranking tables.
Where does the data go?
Nowhere. Everything is processed 100% in your browser. Your CSVs never leave your device. No server, no storage, no tracking.
Why is my chart empty?
Make sure you uploaded the Dates CSV (with columns Date, Clicks, Impressions, CTR, Position). Without it, the timeline chart can't render.
