Frequently Asked Questions

How does this voice search optimizer work?
It analyzes your content for voice search signals: question words, featured snippet paragraphs (40-60 words), readability (Flesch score), conversational tone, and local intent. All analysis runs in your browser.
What is a featured snippet and why is it important?
A featured snippet is Google's answer box. Voice assistants like Google Voice read them aloud. Optimal length is 40-60 words, structured as a direct answer to a question.
Why does readability matter for voice search?
Voice assistants prefer content written at a 6th-8th grade level (Flesch score 60-80). Complex sentences are harder to speak and understand.
Is my content sent to a server?
No. Everything runs 100% in your browser. Your content never leaves your device.
