Frequently Asked Questions

Why is pixel-width more accurate than character count?
Google truncates titles at ~580px and descriptions at ~990px, not by character count. "iiiiiiii" takes fewer pixels than "MMMMMMMM" — our tool measures real pixels via Canvas API.
How is CTR estimated?
CTR is estimated from real-world industry data: position 1 average 27-31%, with adjustments for title length, description quality, snippet presence, and mobile vs desktop.
Is the AI optimization real AI?
It's rule-based optimization, not LLM. It applies proven SEO techniques: removes filler words, adds power words, ensures proper length, and improves structure.
Can I fetch data from my URL?
Yes! Click "Fetch URL" to pull your real page title and meta description via CORS proxy. Works for most sites.
