Frequently Asked Questions

Does this scan the whole internet?
No. This tool honestly compares texts you provide. It detects internal duplication within a single text, matches between two texts, or matches between two URLs. For web-wide plagiarism detection, use Turnitin, Copyscape, or Grammarly.
What algorithms are used?
We use 5 similarity algorithms: Jaccard (word overlap), Cosine (vector similarity), N-gram (phrase matching), Levenshtein (edit distance), and Jaro-Winkler (fuzzy match). Sentence-level matching also uses fuzzy comparison.
Is my text stored or shared?
No. Everything runs 100% in your browser. Your text never leaves your device. No server, no API, no storage, no tracking of content.
Is this really free?
Yes, 100% free forever. No signup, no limits, no hidden costs. Because it runs entirely in your browser, there are no server costs to pass on.
