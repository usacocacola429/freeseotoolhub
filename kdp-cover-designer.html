How the KDP cover size is calculated

Amazon KDP builds a paperback cover as one single wrap file: back cover, spine and front cover side by side, with bleed all the way around.

  • Width = 0.125" bleed + back cover + spine + front cover + 0.125" bleed
  • Height = 0.125" bleed + trim height + 0.125" bleed
  • Spine = page count × paper thickness (white 0.002252", cream 0.0025")

For an 8.5" × 8.5" book with 34 pages on white paper that gives a spine of 0.077" and a full cover of 17.327" × 8.75".

Keep these clear

  • Bleed — 0.125" on every side. Art must reach it, nothing important may sit in it.
  • Safe zone — keep every word at least 0.25" inside the trim edge.
  • Barcode — KDP prints one in the bottom-right of the back cover. Leave a 2" × 1.2" box empty there.
  • Spine text — KDP advises none below 79 pages. A thin spine looks cleaner left blank.
