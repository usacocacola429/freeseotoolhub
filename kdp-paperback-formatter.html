Frequently Asked Questions

Is this free?

Yes, 100% free forever. No signup, no watermarks, no limits.

What is bleed?

Bleed is 0.125" extra space on outer edges so images can extend to the edge after trimming. Required by KDP if your images touch page edges.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. Everything happens locally in your browser. Files never leave your device.

What file formats can I upload?

PDF, JPG, PNG. Multiple files combine into a single KDP-ready PDF.

What is the minimum page count?

KDP requires minimum 24 pages for paperback. For kids books, we recommend 24-32 pages.

Can I use it for Kindle eBook too?

No — this is for paperback. Use our PDF to EPUB Converter for Kindle eBook format.
