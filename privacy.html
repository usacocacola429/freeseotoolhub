1. Introduction

FreeSEOToolHub.com ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (https://freeseotoolhub.com).

By using our free SEO tools, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We have built our platform on the principle of data minimization — most tools work without ever sending your data to our servers.

Our Core Privacy Principle: Client-Side Processing

Most of our tools use client-side processing. Your data (text, keywords, URLs) never leaves your browser. It is processed locally on your device, giving you complete control and privacy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all users. Here's what we collect and why:

2.1 Information Collected Automatically

  • Log Data: Your browser automatically sends standard log data — IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time of visit.
  • Cookies: We use essential cookies for functionality and analytics cookies (via Google Analytics) to understand site usage. You can control cookies via your browser settings.

2.2 Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Contact Information: If you reach out via our Contact page, we collect your name, email, and message to respond to your inquiry.
  • Tool Input Data: Data entered into our tools is processed client-side and never stored on our servers.
Data Type Purpose How It's Protected
IP Address & Browser Data Security, analytics, performance Encrypted logs, auto-deleted after 30 days
Tool Input (Text, URLs) To allow tools to function Client-side only; never stored on our servers
Contact Information To respond to inquiries Encrypted storage; access limited to essential personnel

3. Third-Party Services

We use a limited number of trusted third-party services to operate and improve our website:

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand website traffic and usage patterns. It uses cookies to collect non-personally identifiable information. Learn more about how Google uses data.

Google AdSense

To keep our tools free, we display ads via Google AdSense. AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on prior visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising via Google's Ads Settings.

Note: These third-party services have their own independent privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them. We have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, their practices.

4. How We Protect Your Data

The security of your data is paramount. We implement administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal information.

Our Security Measures Include:

  • Full HTTPS Encryption: Our entire site is secured via industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption.
  • Data Minimization: We collect only what's necessary. Most user data isn't collected at all thanks to client-side architecture.
  • Regular Security Audits: We routinely review our codebase and dependencies for vulnerabilities.
  • Restricted Access: Access to collected personal data is strictly limited to authorized personnel.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location (EU, UK, California), you may have certain rights regarding your personal data. We extend these to all users globally:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request restriction of processing under certain conditions.
  • Right to Object: Object to our processing of your data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request transfer of your data to another organization.

GDPR & CCPA Compliance

For users in the EU/EEA, we comply with GDPR. For California residents, we comply with CCPA. For any other region, we apply the same high standards by default.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us via our Contact page. We respond to all requests within a reasonable timeframe.
