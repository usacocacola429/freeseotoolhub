Frequently Asked Questions

What is keyword density?
Keyword density = (number of keyword occurrences / total words) × 100. Ideal range is 0.5%-2.5% for English, 1%-2.5% for Hindi.
How does Hindi support work?
We use Unicode-aware tokenization, so Hindi (Devanagari), Urdu (Arabic script), and other non-Latin scripts work correctly. No word-boundary issues.
What are "Top Keywords"?
They're automatically extracted from your content using frequency + TF-IDF analysis, without needing you to specify keywords manually.
Is my content private?
Yes, 100%. Everything runs in your browser. No server, no storage, no tracking of your content.
