Frequently Asked Questions

How does this crawler work?
Fetches your homepage via CORS proxy, extracts internal links, then crawls each page up to the selected depth. Analyzes titles, meta descriptions, H1s, images, links, and more — all in your browser.
What issues are detected?
Missing titles/descriptions, duplicate content, missing H1s, multiple H1s, images without alt, missing canonical tags, noindex directives, broken internal links, missing Open Graph tags, missing sitemap, robots.txt issues, and more.
Why does crawl sometimes fail?
Some websites block CORS proxies or have firewalls. We try 3 different proxies. If all fail, try a different website or wait a minute.
Is my data private?
Yes. Everything runs 100% in your browser. We don't store or log any crawled data on our servers.
